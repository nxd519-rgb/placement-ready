PROPOSED UNIVERSITY PILOT · INDEPENDENT STUDENT PROJECT

Helping students prepare for recruitment with confidence

PlacementReady is a student-led prototype designed to reduce unfamiliarity with online recruitment tasks. We propose a voluntary four-week pilot with 20–50 students, subject to careers team approval.

Accessible practice

Free access during the proposed pilot, adjustable difficulty, clear instructions and repeatable exercises.

Student development

Track personal practice accuracy and completion time. No employer pass predictions or personality classifications.

Evaluate the pilot

Invite voluntary anonymous feedback on confidence, usability and perceived usefulness before and after practice. No proven hiring outcome claims.

Privacy and limitations

Practice history is stored in this browser. Optional camera recordings are processed locally and discarded when leaving the exercise. No university account integration or central student dashboard is implemented. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request logs. Accessibility and institutional data protection review are required before a student rollout.

Independent of the University of York, Arctic Shores and HireVue; no endorsement or affiliation is implied. This is a familiarisation tool, not a validated psychometric assessment.
